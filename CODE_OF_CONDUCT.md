# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, useful environment for everyone contributing to MathMesh
Governor. Participants are expected to:

- Be considerate and respectful in discussion and review.
- Critique ideas and code without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy; do not publish credentials, private reports, or user data.
- Make room for different experience levels and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information without
permission.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. They
will review reports, protect the privacy of those involved, and take action that
may include removing content or restricting participation. Maintainers are
expected to apply these standards consistently.
